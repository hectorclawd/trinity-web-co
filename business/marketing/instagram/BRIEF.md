## Intent

The brand kit and shared pieces for 20 Instagram pieces (7 Reels, 9 carousels, 4 story sets), each in English and Spanish. The full plan, scripts and rules are in Hector's "Instagram content plan" doc. The Apple design skill shapes layout, spacing, type scale and motion; the brand's fonts and colors win.

## Assets

- `brand/logo-mark.svg`: the bridge logo, from `site/public/logo-mark.svg`.
- `brand/river.svg` and `brand/generated.js`: the river drawing, from `site/src/components/site/RiverHero.astro`.
- `brand/fonts/`: Archivo, from the site's `@fontsource-variable/archivo`.

## Customizations

- Reel end card, caption track, carousel slide frame and story frame as shared sub-compositions, with Instagram's safe zones drawn on request (`guides`).
- Review samples before any full piece: R1's end card in English and Spanish, and C1's cover.

## Notes

- Nobody appears on camera. Voiceover is ElevenLabs, with Instagram's AI info label on when posted.
- Gold only for primary buttons. No invented social proof, no result promises, no fake urgency.
- `[score]` and `[load time]` stay placeholders until launch day.
