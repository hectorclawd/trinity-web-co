# Instagram content plan: first 20 pieces (draft)

Draft 2026-10-15. Nothing here is built yet. Open questions for Hector are at the end.

## The goal

When a Dallas shop owner gets a walk-in, email or DM from Hector (playbook §7) and taps through to Instagram, the grid should answer three questions in a few seconds: is this a real local person, can he actually build good sites, and what does it cost? The posts back up outreach. They aren't expected to bring in clients on their own.

## Rules every piece follows

From `CLAUDE.md` and the playbook:

- **Prices only from playbook §2–3.** Starter $1,500, Growth $2,800, Premium $4,800. Founding prices $900 / $1,800 for the first 3 clients only. Care plans $49 / $99 / $199.
- **No invented social proof.** No testimonials, client counts or "trusted by" lines. Every demo is labeled "Demo" on screen and in the caption.
- **No result promises.** Never say "page 1", "more calls guaranteed" or "rank higher". Say what gets built and how it's measured.
- **No fake urgency.** The founding offer says "first 3 clients" and shows spots left only if that's true on the day it posts. No "this month".
- **Don't lead with AI.** No piece is about AI tools.
- **Real numbers only.** The demos have no production PageSpeed scores yet, so any score or load time appears as `[score]` / `[load time]` until launch day. No borrowed stats ("53% of visitors leave…") unless we can cite a current source.
- **Never mock a real business's site.** "Slow site" examples use a made-up generic template.
- **Brand:** Archivo (wide headlines, normal-width body), Ink / River / Limestone / Paper, with Gold only for the founding offer and primary buttons. The river line drawing and bridge logo are recurring motifs. Voice: plain, friendly, sentence case.

## The 20 pieces

### 1. Reel: "A web developer you can text"
- **Hook (0–2 s):** "Your website guy shouldn't be a ticket number."
- **Scenes:** text bubbles pop in ("Can you change our Saturday hours?" → "Done, take a look"). Then the river line drawing draws across Dallas neighborhoods. Last frame: "Hector, Dallas. Fast websites for local businesses."
- **Caption:** who Hector is, who he builds for (trades, barbers, salons), and that he answers texts the same day.
- **CTA:** "DM me or tap the link in bio."
- **Needs:** headshot or a short phone clip of Hector (optional, see questions).

### 2. Carousel: "What a website costs here (no 'call for a quote')"
- **Slides:** cover → Starter $1,500 (up to 4 pages) → Growth $2,800 (up to 7 pages, booking, quote form; "most local businesses fit here") → Premium $4,800 (up to 12 pages, blog) → "Every build includes 3 months of care" → "You own your domain and accounts" → CTA.
- **Caption:** why fixed prices, and what's included vs. an add-on.

### 3. Carousel: "Founding clients: 3 spots"
- **Slides:** "I'm taking 3 founding clients" → Starter $900 / Growth $1,800 → "What I ask in return: a written testimonial and permission to show your site as a case study" → "That's it. No Google review trade" → "[X] of 3 spots left" (only if true) → CTA.
- **Uses Gold.** Posts only after the business name, LLC and contracts are ready (see questions).

### 4. Reel: "The 1-second test"
- **Hook:** "Pull up your website on your phone. Count."
- **Scenes:** two phones side by side. Left: a generic made-up template, with a spinner, pop-up and layout shift. Right: the Purple Martin demo loading in `[load time]`, labeled "Demo". End on the PageSpeed gauge at `[score]`.
- **Caption:** people searching "near me" are on a phone and call whoever loads first (playbook wording, no made-up stat).
- **Waits on:** production PageSpeed numbers (launch day). Can be built now with placeholders.

### 5. Carousel: "5 reasons your site is slow on phones"
- **Slides:** giant uncompressed photos → page builders loading code for features you don't use → too many plugins and widgets → autoplay video and sliders → pop-ups on top of pop-ups → "How I build instead: hand-coded, about 1 second on a phone" → CTA "Want me to check yours? DM 'CHECK'".

### 6. Story set: "How fast is your site?"
- **Frames:** poll sticker "How long does your site take on your phone?" (Under 2 s / 2–5 s / 5 s+ / I don't have one) → "Here's how to check in 30 seconds: pagespeed.web.dev" → screen-recorded steps → "Under 50? DM me 'CHECK' and I'll send a free 2-minute video with 3 fixes."
- Ties straight into the §7 Loom offer.

### 7. Carousel: "What a customer looks for in the first 5 seconds"
- **Slides:** a tap-to-call button → hours and "open now" → where you work (service area) → prices or "starting at" → real reviews → a way to book or get a quote → "Most sites I see miss at least 3" → CTA.
- Each slide shows that element on a demo, labeled "Demo".

### 8. Carousel: "7 free Google Business Profile fixes you can do today"
- **Slides:** pick the right primary category → add services with prices → hours, including holidays → 10+ real photos → website link with the right page → reply to every review → post once a week → "Want help? It's part of Growth" → CTA.
- A useful free post that also makes sense to share.

### 9. Reel: "What happens when someone searches 'AC repair near me'"
- **Scenes:** typed search → map results → tap a profile → the website opens → tap to call. Each step gets a line: "This is where most local sites lose people." End: "I build the website, the profile setup and the review links as one job."
- No promise of ranking.

### 10. Reel: "Demo: Purple Martin Heating & Air"
- **Hook:** "I built a site for a made-up HVAC company to show you how I'd build yours."
- **Scenes:** phone scroll-through with callouts: the Call/Text bar, "How a visit works", compact services, service area. "Demo" badge on screen the whole time.
- **Caption:** says plainly that it's a demo for a business that doesn't exist.

### 11. Reel: "Demo: Good Oak Barbershop"
- **Scenes:** price menu, text-first booking, "Open now" badge, gallery. "Demo" badge the whole time.
- **Hook:** "Your Instagram is great. Here's what your website could do that Instagram can't."

### 12. Carousel: "Why every section on this demo is there"
- **Slides:** annotated screenshots of the Good Oak (or Purple Martin) demo, one design decision per slide ("The call button follows you down the page because…").
- Shows thinking, not just looks.

### 13. Carousel: "4 things you should own (not your web guy)"
- **Slides:** your domain → your Google Business Profile → your site's code → your analytics → "If someone else owns these, leaving gets expensive" → "With me, you own all four from day one" → CTA.

### 14. Carousel: "Agency vs. DIY vs. a local developer"
- **Slides:** built from the playbook §1 positioning table: upfront cost, monthly cost, who builds it, speed on phones, turnaround, ownership. One row per slide, then a summary.
- **Line to use:** "Wix is great if you have the time. Most owners would rather spend it on jobs."

### 15. Carousel: "What $99 a month actually covers"
- **Slides:** hosting and SSL → monthly form and booking checks → security updates and backups → 1 hour of edits → a one-page monthly report (visits, calls, forms) → 2 Google Business Profile posts → "Need less? $49. More? $199" → CTA.

### 16. Reel: "From first call to launch"
- **Scenes:** an animated timeline along the river line: 20-minute call → proposal in 24 hours → deposit and intake → build → 2 review rounds → launch checklist → handoff. "2 to 5 weeks, depending on the package."

### 17. Story set: "Behind the build"
- **Frames:** 4–5 frames from a real build day: a sketch, code on screen, a phone test, a QA checklist being ticked. Quiz sticker: "Which loads faster?"
- Uses the demos until there's a client.

### 18. Carousel: "7 questions to ask before you hire a web designer"
- **Slides:** Who owns the domain? → What's the monthly cost, and what's included? → How many revision rounds? → Who answers when something breaks? → Can I leave, and what does it cost? → How fast is it on a phone? → Can I see sites you've built? → "My answers: link in bio."

### 19. Story set: "Ask me anything about your website"
- **Frames:** question sticker → answer frames over the next days (each answer a branded frame) → saved to a "FAQ" highlight.

### 20. Story set: "Free 2-minute site check"
- **Frames:** "Send me your website (or your Facebook page)" → "I'll record a 2-minute video: how it looks on a phone, its speed score, 3 fixes" → "Free. No pitch unless you ask." → DM sticker / "DM 'CHECK'".
- Save to a "Free check" highlight. This is the main lead source for the account.

## How they get built (once the tools are in)

- One HyperFrames project in this repo (for example `business/marketing/instagram/`) with shared brand tokens (fonts, colors, river drawing, logo, "Demo" badge) and one composition per piece.
- Every business fact (name, handle, prices, `[score]` placeholders) comes from one config file, like `site.ts` does for sites, so a rename or new PageSpeed numbers update all 20 at once.
- Demo footage: Playwright screen recordings of the demo previews at phone size.
- The Apple design skill guides layout, spacing and motion. Brand fonts and colors stay ours (see question 4).
