## 2. What's included

**Care:**
- Hosting and SSL certificate
- Uptime monitoring
- Monthly backups
- Security and software fixes
- Up to 30 minutes of small edits per month (text, photos, hours, prices)

**Care Plus** adds:
- Up to 2 hours of edits per month
- One Google Business Profile post per month
- Quarterly traffic and search report

Unused edit time doesn't roll over. Work beyond the plan, including new pages or new features, is quoted separately or billed at $[75]/hour after Client approves it.

## 3. Response times

Designer responds to edit requests within 2 business days and completes small edits within 5 business days. If the site goes down, Designer will begin work on it within 1 business day.

## 4. Billing

The plan is billed monthly in advance by card or ACH. If a payment fails and isn't fixed within 14 days, Designer may pause the plan.

## 5. Cancellation

Either party may cancel with 30 days' written notice. On cancellation, Designer will give Client a full copy of the website files and help move hosting to Client's own account. Client keeps the domain.

## 6. Limits

Designer isn't responsible for downtime caused by the hosting provider, the domain registrar or other third parties, beyond working with them to fix it. Designer doesn't guarantee search rankings, traffic or sales. Liability is limited to the fees Client paid in the 3 months before the claim.
