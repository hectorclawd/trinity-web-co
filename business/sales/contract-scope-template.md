## 4. Revisions and change orders

- **One revision round** is a single consolidated list of changes, sent within 5 business days of the review link.
- New pages, new features or a new design direction are **change orders**, quoted in writing and approved before any work begins.
- Extra revision rounds are $150 each. Other extra work is billed at $75/hour in 30-minute blocks, quoted first.

## 5. Client responsibilities

The Client will:

- Provide all content (logo, photos, text, reviews) by the content due date.
- Make sure all claims, prices, licenses and certifications shown on the site are accurate and current.
- Own or have the right to use every photo, logo and piece of text they send, and accept responsibility for it.
- Name one person to approve the site and give consolidated feedback.

## 6. Ownership

- Once paid in full, the Client owns the final site code, content and domain.
- The Designer keeps the right to reuse its own templates, components and know-how on other projects.
- The Designer may show the finished site in its portfolio.
- **The Client always owns their domain**, registered in the Client's own registrar account. The Client also owns their Google Business Profile and Google Search Console; the Designer is added as a manager or user, never the only owner. Visitor stats from the hosting analytics are shared on request.
- Logins are shared through a password manager, never by email or text.

**Care plan:** ☐ Care plan $________/month ☐ Declined (handoff at launch)

- The care plan starts billing on launch day (________) and renews every month at **$________/month**, billed on the ____ of each month by auto-charge. There are no free or included months.
- Included: ________ *(copy from playbook §3)*. Content edits are not included: every edit, and any other work beyond the plan, is $75/hour in 30-minute blocks, quoted first.
- **Either side may cancel with 30 days' written notice.**
- **On cancellation:** the Designer transfers the GitHub repository to the Client's account and helps move hosting, for a one-time $150 handoff fee. The Designer keeps hosting the site for 30 days after the plan ends so the Client can move it.
- **Late payments:** if a care-plan payment isn't fixed within 15 days, edits pause, but the site stays online. If it's still unpaid 60 days after its due date, the Designer may end the care plan by written notice and hand off the site. The unpaid fees and the handoff fee stay owed (master service agreement §7.6a and §7.8).
- Price increases require 60 days' written notice.

## 8. Abandonment

If the Client stops responding for 30 days or more (including content more than 30 days late), the Designer may invoice the remaining balance and either close the project or launch the site with the content on hand.

## 9. Warranty

For 30 days after launch, the Designer fixes bugs (things that don't work as built) at no charge. New requests and content changes are not bugs.

## 10. Limitation of liability

- The Designer's total liability is capped at the fees the Client has paid under this agreement.
- **The Designer does not guarantee search rankings, traffic, leads or revenue.**

## 11. Termination

- Either side may end the project with written notice.
- The deposit is fully refundable until the kickoff call and non-refundable after it. *(Attorney to confirm.)*
- After the kickoff call, the Client pays the greater of the deposit or the work completed up to the termination date at $75/hour, never more than the total above. Any amount paid beyond that is refunded (master service agreement §11.3).

## 12. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Venue is Dallas County, Texas.
