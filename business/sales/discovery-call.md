# Discovery call script (20 minutes)

Source: playbook §4. Goal: find out if they're a fit, then send a proposal **within 24 hours**.

**Before the call (5 min):** pull up their current site on your phone, run PageSpeed (mobile), check their Google Business Profile and review count. Have the price sheet open.

---

**Open (1 min)**
> Thanks for making time. I'll ask a few questions about your business so I can recommend the right package. Takes about 20 minutes, and you'll have a written proposal from me by tomorrow. Sound good?

### Business and goals (8 min)

1. Walk me through what you do and who your best customer is.
2. How do most new customers find you today? *(Google, referrals, Facebook, Yelp, Nextdoor)*
3. If the new site works, what happens? More calls, more bookings, higher-priced jobs, fewer "what do you charge?" calls?
4. Roughly what is one new customer worth to you? *(This is how you justify the price.)*
5. What areas or neighborhoods do you serve?

### Current setup (4 min)

6. Do you have a website now? Who built it and who has the logins?
7. Do you own your domain? Which company is it registered with?
8. Do you have a Google Business Profile? How many reviews?
9. What do you use for booking, payments or quotes?

### Fit and logistics (5 min)

10. Show me 2–3 websites you like, from any industry. What do you like about them?
11. Do you have a logo, photos of your work, and text about your services?
12. When do you want this live, and is anything driving that date?
13. Who besides you has a say in the decision?
14. Have you set a budget?
    *If they hesitate:* "My packages run $1,500 to $4,800. Most businesses like yours fit Growth at $2,800. For my first 3 clients only, I have founding prices: $900 for Starter or $1,800 for Growth."
    *(Drop the founding sentence once those spots are filled.)*

### Close (2 min)

> Based on what you told me, I'd recommend **[package]**. I'll send a proposal by **[tomorrow, time]** with the exact pages, price and timeline. If it looks right, you sign online and pay a deposit to hold your spot: half the price, or 40% for Premium. Any questions before then?

- Pitch **Growth first**. Starter is the fallback if budget is tight.
- Don't promise rankings or results. Say what you'll build and how you'll measure it.

---

### Red flags: walk away or price higher

- [ ] Wants e-commerce with 100+ products
- [ ] "Just copy this competitor's site"
- [ ] Asks for unlimited revisions
- [ ] Can't name who decides
- [ ] Pushes back on any deposit

### After the call

- [ ] Log it in `prospect-tracker.csv` (status: Call done, next follow-up: proposal date)
- [ ] Send the proposal within 24 hours
- [ ] Follow up on the proposal on day 2 and day 5 (playbook §7, Week 4)
