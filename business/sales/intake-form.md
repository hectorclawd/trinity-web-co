# Client intake form (for Tally)

Source: playbook §4, "Intake form fields." Build this once in Tally and send the link **after the deposit clears**. Each `##` heading below is one Tally page. Field type and required flag are in brackets; leave them out of the question text.

**Form title:** Let's build your website

**Intro text:**
> Thanks for choosing Trinity Web Co. This form gives me everything I need to build your site. It takes about 20–30 minutes. You can save and come back. If you don't have something yet, skip it and we'll sort it out together.

---

## 1. Business basics

1. What is your legal business name? [Short answer · required]
2. What name do customers know you by? (If it's the same, type "same.") [Short answer · required]
3. What phone number should customers call or text? [Phone · required]
4. Which email address should website form leads go to? [Email · required]
5. What is your street address? If you go to your customers and don't want an address shown, type "service-area only." [Long answer · required]
6. What are your business hours? [Long answer · required]
7. What year did you start the business? [Number]
8. If you're in a licensed trade, list your license numbers (for example, TACLA or TSBPE numbers). [Long answer]

## 2. Services

9. List each service you offer, with 1–2 sentences about each one. [Long answer · required]
10. Do you show starting prices publicly? If yes, list them. [Long answer]
11. Which 3 services do you most want to feature? [Long answer · required]

## 3. Service area

12. Which cities and neighborhoods do you serve? [Long answer · required]
13. What's the farthest you'll drive for a job? [Short answer]

## 4. Your customers

14. Describe your ideal customer. [Long answer · required]
15. What are the top 3 questions customers ask you? [Long answer · required]
16. What are the top 3 reasons people choose you over someone else? [Long answer · required]

## 5. Brand

17. Upload your logo files (the highest quality you have). [File upload · multiple]
18. Do you have brand colors? If yes, list them or describe them. [Short answer]
19. Share 3 websites you like (any industry) and what you like about each. [Long answer · required]
20. What words describe your business? (For example: friendly, fast, family-owned.) [Short answer · required]

## 6. Content

21. Upload 10–30 photos of your work, your team or your place of business. [File upload · multiple]
22. Team bios: who should appear on the site, and a few lines about each person. [Long answer]
23. Paste 5–10 of your favorite customer reviews, with the customer's first name as it appears on the review. [Long answer · required]
24. List any certifications or awards. [Long answer]

## 7. Accounts

25. Where is your domain registered (for example, GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare)? If you don't have one yet, type "none." [Short answer · required]
26. Do you have a Google Business Profile? If yes, I'll send you a manager invite to accept. [Multiple choice: Yes / No / Not sure · required]
27. What booking tool do you use, if any (Square, Vagaro, Booksy, Calendly, Cal.com)? Paste your booking link. [Short answer]
28. Paste links to your social media pages. [Long answer]

> Don't type any passwords in this form. I'll send you a secure way to share logins.

## 8. Legal

29. Your site will include a privacy policy page, since it collects contact form info. Is there anything specific it needs to mention? [Long answer]
30. Does your industry require any disclaimers on your website? If yes, paste the wording. [Long answer]

## 9. Sign-off

31. Who will approve the site before launch? [Short answer · required]
32. What's the best way to reach that person? [Multiple choice: Text / Phone call / Email · required]
33. Which days work best for you to review the site? [Checkboxes: Mon / Tue / Wed / Thu / Fri / Sat / Sun]

**Thank-you page:**
> Got it, thank you! I'll review everything and send you a one-page site plan to approve before I start designing. Your content is due **[content due date]**. If anything's missing, I'll let you know.
