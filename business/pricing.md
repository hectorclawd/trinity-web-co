# Pricing

> **Status: proposed starting prices (2026-10-07).** Hector to confirm. When anything here changes, update the pricing section of `site/index.html` to match.

Prices are fixed per package, not hourly. Quote "from" prices publicly; the final quote comes after the discovery call.

## Website packages (one-time)

| Package | Price | Best for | Includes | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch** | from $1,200 | New businesses, food trucks, solo pros | One scrolling page: services, about, hours and map, contact form. Mobile-first. Google Business Profile setup or cleanup. 1 round of revisions. | 1–2 weeks |
| **Storefront** | from $2,800 | Most local businesses | Up to 6 pages. Help writing your copy. Local search setup (titles, descriptions, schema, Google Business Profile). Booking, menu or quote-request integration. 2 rounds of revisions. | 3–4 weeks |
| **Growth** | from $5,200 | Multi-service or multi-location businesses | Up to 12 pages, including a page per service or location. Everything in Storefront, plus a reviews section, analytics setup, and 3 months of local search check-ins. 3 rounds of revisions. | 5–6 weeks |

### What drives the price up from "from"
- More pages than the package allows (see add-ons)
- Writing all the copy from scratch with no source material
- Online store with more than ~10 products (quote separately or refer out to Shopify)
- Complex integrations (custom booking logic, member logins)

## Care plans (monthly)

Optional. Clients who skip the care plan get a full handoff: files, domain and hosting moved to accounts in their name.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Care** | $59/mo | Hosting, SSL, uptime monitoring, monthly backups, security fixes, up to 30 min of small edits per month |
| **Care Plus** | $129/mo | Everything in Care, plus up to 2 hours of edits per month, monthly Google Business Profile post, quarterly traffic and search report |

Unused edit time doesn't roll over. Work beyond the plan is billed at the hourly rate.

## Founding-client offer

First 3 clients: **30% off any website package**, in exchange for:
1. An honest Google review once the site launches
2. Permission to show the site and a short case study on trinitywebco.com

Remove this offer from the site once the 3 spots are taken.

## Payment terms

- **50% deposit** to book the project, **50% at launch** (before the site goes live on the client's domain).
- Care plans billed monthly in advance by card or ACH. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.
- Invoices due within 7 days. Work pauses on invoices more than 14 days late.
- No discounts beyond the founding-client offer. If budget is tight, drop scope (smaller package) rather than price.

## Sanity check against the market

Dallas freelancers typically charge about $1,500–$6,000 for a small-business site. Local agencies charge $5,000–$15,000+. DIY builders (Wix, Squarespace) cost $200–$500/yr plus the owner's time. These prices put us at the affordable end of custom work, with the care plan as recurring revenue. Revisit after the first 5 projects: if every quote gets a "yes" with no pushback, prices are too low.
