# Walk-in and phone scripts

Walk-ins work best for restaurants, salons, shops and other places with a counter. Go at a slow time: 2–4 pm on weekdays, never during a rush. Bring business cards and a one-page printout of the audit for that business.

## Walk-in

> Hi, is the owner or manager around? ... Hi, I'm Hector. I build websites for local businesses here in Dallas. I'm not here to sell you anything today. I was looking at [BUSINESS NAME] on my phone and noticed [ONE SPECIFIC THING]. I wrote down a few quick fixes, and you're welcome to keep this. *(Hand over the printout and card.)* If you ever want help with it, my number's on there.

**If they're interested:**
> Great. Can I grab 20 minutes with you sometime this week to hear about the business? I'll come back, or we can do a phone call, whatever's easiest.

**If they're busy:**
> No problem at all. I'll leave this with you. Mind if I check back in a couple of weeks?

**If they say they already have someone:**
> Good, that's the most important thing. Keep the list anyway, it might be useful for them.

Log the visit in `clients/_leads.md` the same day.

## Phone

> Hi, this is Hector with Trinity Web Co. I'm a web designer here in Dallas. Is this a bad time? ... I'll be quick. I was looking at [BUSINESS NAME] online and noticed [SPECIFIC THING]. I help local businesses fix exactly that kind of thing. Would it be worth a 20-minute call later this week to see if I can help?

**Voicemail (under 20 seconds):**
> Hi [NAME], this is Hector with Trinity Web Co. here in Dallas. I noticed [SPECIFIC THING] about your website and had a couple of ideas to fix it. Call or text me back at [NUMBER] if you're interested. Thanks!

## Common objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I get all my customers from word of mouth." | That's the best kind. When someone gets your name from a friend, the first thing most people do is look you up. The website is what they find. |
| "It's too expensive." | Totally fair. The one-page Launch site starts at $1,200, and it's the same quality, just smaller. We can always add to it later. |
| "My nephew is doing it." | Good. If he gets stuck or busy, I'm happy to help. |
| "I have a Facebook page." | Keep it! But Facebook doesn't show up well in Google searches, and some customers don't use Facebook. The site sends people to both. |
| "Can you guarantee I'll be #1 on Google?" | No, and anyone who does is guessing. What I can promise is that your site will be set up the way Google expects, which most local sites aren't. |
