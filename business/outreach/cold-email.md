# Cold email

**Rules**
- **One business, one email, one real observation.** Never send a mass blast. Do the 10-minute audit from the playbook first, and name a specific problem you found.
- Keep it under 120 words. One question at the end, no links except your site.
- **CAN-SPAM:** every commercial email needs a real mailing address and an easy way to opt out. Use the footer below. Honour opt-outs immediately and log them in `clients/_leads.md`.
- Send from `hello@trinitywebco.com`, Tuesday to Thursday mornings.

---
