# Follow-ups

Most yeses come on the second or third touch. Stop after three. Never guilt-trip.

## After a cold email, no reply

**Day 4:**
> Hi [FIRST NAME], just floating this back up in case it got buried. Happy to send over the 3 fixes I'd make to [BUSINESS NAME]'s site, no call needed. — Hector

**Day 11 (last one):**
> Hi [FIRST NAME], I'll leave it here so I don't clutter your inbox. If a new website ever makes it onto the list, I'm at hello@trinitywebco.com. Good luck with everything at [BUSINESS NAME]. — Hector

## After sending a proposal, no reply

**Day 3:**
> Hi [FIRST NAME], did the proposal come through OK? Happy to walk through it on a quick call, or adjust anything that doesn't fit. — Hector

**Day 7:**
> Hi [FIRST NAME], checking in on the proposal. If the timing or budget isn't right just now, no problem. Would it help if I trimmed it down to the Launch package to start? — Hector

**Day 14 (last one):**
> Hi [FIRST NAME], I'll close this out on my end for now. If anything changes, just reply to this email and we'll pick it right back up. — Hector

## After launch (review request)

**Within 7 days of launch:**
> Hi [FIRST NAME], it was great working on [BUSINESS NAME]'s new site. If you've been happy with how it went, would you leave a short Google review? It makes a huge difference for a small business like mine: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]. Thank you! — Hector

## Referral ask (90 days after launch)

> Hi [FIRST NAME], hope the new site's been treating you well. Quick question: do you know any other business owners who could use a hand with their website? I'd take great care of anyone you send my way. — Hector
