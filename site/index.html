Questions owners ask

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain stays in your own account, and once the project is paid in full, the code, words and photos made for your site are yours. Your Google Business Profile stays in your name too. I'm added as a manager, never the only owner.

What do I need to have ready?

Your logo (if you have one), some photos, a list of your services and your hours. If you're missing any of that, we'll work it out together. I can help write the copy and connect you with a local photographer.

How long does it take?

Starter takes 10 business days, Growth 15 and Premium 25, counted from the day your photos and business details are in. You'll get a checklist and a due date at kickoff, because waiting on content is the most common delay.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes. I'll keep what's working, fix what isn't, and set up redirects from your old pages so you don't lose the Google traffic you already have.

Do I have to sign up for a care plan?

Every build includes the first 3 months of the Standard plan, so you can see what it does. After that it continues at $99/mo, and you can switch plans or cancel with 30 days' notice. If you'd rather host the site yourself, I'll transfer the code to you and help move the hosting for a one-time $150.

Can you guarantee I'll rank first on Google?

No one honestly can. What I can promise is a fast site that's set up the way Google expects, with your business details matching everywhere. Most local sites don't get that far.
